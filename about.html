Our Story

From a Small Kitchen to Your Table.

Tapas & Co. began as a tiny neighbourhood kitchen with one long table, a handful of recipes passed down through generations, and a belief that good food is meant to be shared. We've grown since then — but the heart of what we do hasn't changed.

Every dish still starts with the same care: ingredients chosen by hand, recipes cooked slowly, and plates designed to land in the middle of the table so everyone can reach in.

María & Diego Alvarez Founders
What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision.

Two guiding beliefs that shape every dish we serve and every guest we welcome.

Our Mission

To bring people together around a table with honest, handcrafted Spanish food — served generously, priced fairly, and always worth sharing. We exist to turn a simple meal into a memory.

What We Do Every Day

Our Vision

To become the city's most loved tapas bar — a place where first-time guests become regulars, and where the spirit of Spanish sharing dining feels genuinely alive, night after night.
